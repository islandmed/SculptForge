# 0.10.9 review fixes: what to try
- Default sphere, Ctrl+D twice, then click **Dynamic**: you should get **Freeze Levels and Turn On Dynamic**. Sculpt small Clay / Standard strokes (Draw Size ~19, X symmetry) and say if anything looks lumpy or streaky. Ctrl+Z should bring the levels back.
- Mask part of the model with Wireframe on: is the mask readable at the default **Mask darkness**, and is the wire still visible? Try the slider in Tool > Masking.
- **View > Views**: the six axis buttons and Numpad 1/3/7 (Ctrl for the opposite side) in your input style; save two named views, save the project, reopen, restore them (Numpad 0 = the last one).
- Quad Remesh guides (Tool > Geometry > Quad Remesh > Guides): do curves draw smooth and stand out? Are "on" toggles easy to tell from "off" in your colour scheme?
- Tool > Geometry: Del Lower / Del Higher now ask first; Micro sits in its own group.
- Tool > Geometry: **Divide (keeps levels)** and **Subdivide (removes levels)** now sit in separate groups; on a Part with levels, Subdivide asks first (try **Don't ask again**, and Ctrl+Z afterwards).
