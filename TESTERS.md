# Precision work (0.10.9): what to try
- Press **L** and draw with Dots, Spray, Drag Stamp and Slide Stamp: the brush trails on a string. Let go: does the
  line finish at the cursor? (Stroke picker > Catch up on release turns that off.)
- **Draw > World Size**: set a small brush on a face, zoom in and out. It should stay the same size on the skin, and
  the slider should show px and mm.
- Go tiny: **Shift+[** down to 0.5 px and draw pores and fine wrinkles on a dense mesh. Any gaps or wobble? Also try
  **Ctrl+Shift+right-drag** to scrub the size.
- Start a stroke, then hold **Shift+Alt**: the brush slows to 1/4 speed at Precision Intensity. Tell us which input
  style you use (ZBrush, SculptForge, Blender, Maya) if something else happens.
- Pen users: give a brush the **Fine detail** curve (Brush Lab or Preferences > Tablet), switch brushes and back:
  is the curve kept, and does light pressure feel controllable?
