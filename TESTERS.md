# Discord intake: what to try (once the intake bot is set up)
- Start a new thread in #ideas or #help with a real title and at least 20 characters of text. Within about 30 minutes it should appear as a GitHub issue (label from-discord), and the thread should get a "Thanks! Tracked as #N" reply.
- Post a bug in #bug-reports with an image attached. The issue should show your text as a quote and the image as a link only.
- Post something very short, like "hi" or "+1", or reply to someone else's bug. Neither should become an issue.
- When a maintainer closes your issue, the closing note should appear once in your thread.
