  smaller rings for the opposite ends; the ends pointing away are smaller and fainter, on a soft disc that reads on
  dark and light backgrounds. Click an end for that view, click it again for the opposite view, drag on the disc to
  orbit; an exact view's name ("Front view", "Top view"...) shows under it.
highlight: Clearer corner axes: lettered, depth-shaded, click twice for the opposite view
changed: **Corner axes** (Draw > Camera > Axes): positive ends are filled, lettered circles (X / Y / Z), negative
  ends smaller rings (their letter under the pointer); far ends are smaller and fainter and the nearest is drawn on
  top; a translucent disc behind them (stronger under the pointer) keeps them readable on any background. A second
  click on the end facing you snaps to the opposite view, a drag on the disc orbits, and the name of an exact side
  view shows under the axes. No brush ring over the axes. Small / Medium / Large and the UI scale still apply.
testers:
  - Any OS, dark and light backgrounds (Preferences > Display > Flat Colour, a light grey): the axes read at a glance;
    the end pointing at you is the biggest and on top.
  - Click +Z: Front view (named under the axes); click it again: Back view. Same for X and Y.
  - Drag on the disc between the ends: the view orbits; no brush ring shows over the axes.
  - Windows at 150% and 200% scaling, Draw > Camera > Gizmo size Small / Medium / Large: the letters stay readable
    and every end still clicks.
