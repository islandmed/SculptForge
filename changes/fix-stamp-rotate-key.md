whats_new: **Turn a Slide Stamp as you slide it**: hold **Shift** while dragging a Slide Stamp and the alpha turns toward the cursor instead of the stamp moving; let go and it slides on with that turn.
added: **Turn a Slide Stamp with a key** (#175, thanks @Devon0209): with the Slide Stamp stroke, hold Shift after the drag has begun and the stamp stays where it is while its alpha turns toward the cursor; let go and it slides on with the turn kept. The turn belongs to that stroke and the brush's Angle is not changed. Shift held at the press still smooths. **Edit > Preferences > Input > Slide Stamp rotates with** picks Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Off.
testers:
  - Any OS: Slide Stamp stroke with an alpha (Stroke popup, G), drag, then hold Shift partway: the stamp stops, the alpha turns toward the cursor; let go, it slides on, turned.
  - Hold Shift before pressing: it smooths as before. The brush's Angle slider is unchanged after the stroke.
  - Preferences > Input > Slide Stamp rotates with: Ctrl or Alt, and Off.
