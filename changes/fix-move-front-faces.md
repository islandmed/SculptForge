fixed: "**Move** ignored **Front faces only** (a member's report): with View falloff on, a Move drag on a one-piece head also pulled the back of it, and Connected only could not help there. Move now takes only the surface facing the camera when Front faces only is on, on plain meshes and Micro levels alike (its pull eases off toward the silhouette and toward any surface facing away, such as an ear's back, so the surface there bends instead of tearing); with it off nothing changes. The guide's View falloff row says which to use."
testers:
  - "Any OS: Sphere or a head, Draw > View falloff on, Move with a big brush over the front: the back moves along (as before). Turn Front faces only on (Brush panel): the same drag moves the near side only; the back stays."
  - "Any OS: the same on a Micro level, and with X symmetry: nothing facing away from the camera moves. Front faces only off: Move behaves as before."
