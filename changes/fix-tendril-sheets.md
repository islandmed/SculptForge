whats_new: **Tendril** pulls round horns: with Dynamic on, a long pull stays a round tube instead of thinning into a flat, folded sheet, and with **View falloff** on it no longer drags the far side of the model along.
fixed: **Tendril** with Dynamic on pulled flat, folded sheets out of a coarse Part (reported by Tpqz_ on Discord): the pinch that tapers the horn closed it to a thread thinner than the triangles Dynamic adds, which then folded. With **View falloff** on, Tendril also leaves the surface deeper than about 1.5 brush radii behind the tip alone, as the other brushes do (#229), so a pull no longer drags front and back together into a sheet. Keys and settings are unchanged.
testers:
