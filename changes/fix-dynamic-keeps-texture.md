whats_new: **Dynamic** keeps a painted Part's texture: sculpting with Dynamic on no longer erases the paint.
fixed: Dynamic topology no longer erases a Part's painted texture. The remesh runs exactly as on a Part without UVs; afterwards every triangle it changed takes its UVs from the surface as it was before the dab (closest point, one chart per triangle, drawn in where it would sample outside the painted area), so the paint stays in place, no white specks appear where big brushes simplify a painted head, and Undo restores the UVs exactly.
testers:
  - Windows or macOS: Auto UV the Human head, paint a few colours, turn Dynamic on and drag big Smooth, Standard and Clay brushes across the eyes and forehead; the paint stays, with no white specks or streaks, and painting over grown geometry (a Tendril) covers it.
  - Windows or macOS: paint a Part into its texture (Paint into Texture), turn Dynamic on and sculpt over the paint, big and small strokes, across a seam if the Part has one; the paint stays (right at a seam it can look a little squeezed). Undo, then redo; save and reopen.

Reviewers: src/core/Dyntopo.cpp (saveUv / transferUvs / labelCharts / buildUvMask; per-slot UVs in TopologyTriangleValues), src/render/Renderer.cpp (patches carry their triangles' UVs), tests/test_dyntopo.cpp, tests/e2e/dynamic_keeps_texture.sfui. The paint notice (asks once before the first stroke) is removed: there is nothing to warn about.
Design: no UV rule touches the topology (the first attempts refused collapses, flips and relaxes near seams and bent charts; that bent the geometry: hubs, folds, torn rings). A dab saves each triangle it changes (and those around a vertex it moves) before changing it; after the operations every changed triangle's corners take the UV of their closest point on that saved surface, a corner that did not move keeps its UV, and a triangle whose corners land in different charts takes all three from one (the one most corners are in; near-ties do not vote). A triangle that comes out turned over takes the affine map of the chart's triangle at its middle; one that would sample outside the 1024 footprint of the charts (kept from stroke to stroke) is drawn in towards the chart.
Measured, six stroke lines on the Human head with Auto UV (laptop arm64): the mesh (positions + triangles) is identical to the run without UVs on all six; folded edges equal (medians 76 / 76); triangles with UVs outside every chart 0-19 of 9k-21k (bar 100); cost 0.5-0.6 ms per dab over 2.4-3.4 ms (+15-20%). Known: 8-20% of triangles near seams have a texture density 50x off the median (they take one side's map): artist-qa judges the paint at seams.
