whats_new: **Pen pressure on Linux**: Wayland pens keep their pressure through the whole stroke, the eraser end works on Wayland, and **Edit > Preferences > Tablet > Troubleshooting details** shows what the pen sends, with **Copy details** for a bug report.
fixed: **Pen pressure on Wayland and X11** (Huguillon on YouTube): the Linux packages now carry SDL 3.4.18 (was 3.2.30) and the AUR package needs it: a Wayland pen no longer counts as a new pen each time it comes into range, and its eraser end inverts as set in Preferences > Tablet instead of drawing as the tip. A stroke sample's dabs now get that sample's own pressure: SDL sends the motion before the pressure (every platform), and the stroke took the sample before's. On Linux a sample whose pressure SDL lost (X11 valuators left out, a Wayland frame with a hover distance) keeps the last pressure instead of dropping to 0 mid-stroke; a NaN or infinite pressure is ignored everywhere. Touch events SDL made from the pen are off, so a finger on a pen display beside the pen no longer ends the stroke as a two-finger gesture.
added: **Preferences > Tablet > Troubleshooting details**: the SDL version, the video driver (wayland or x11 on Linux), the session and package, the pen's events since launch, lost pressure samples, the system's own mouse events while the pen was in range, the last pressure and the last stroke's range; **Copy details** copies them. Running through XWayland, it says how to start SculptForge on Wayland (`SDL_VIDEO_DRIVER=wayland`).
