whats_new: **Founding Members and Member Requests**: the first 100 members are **Founding Members** for good (a
  badge, their name in the credits, their price locked for as long as they stay), and each month every member can
  ask for a brush, an alpha or a material in **#member-requests** on Discord: we make it, credited to them. A small
  membership card shows now and then at a good moment (never mid-stroke, at most once a week); members sign in once
  with **I'm already a member** and it never shows again.
changed: Membership: **Member Requests** (one request per member per month: brushes, alphas, materials, small
  tweaks; "Requested by @name" on each item; out to members at month end, free for everyone a month later) replace
  the monthly brush and alpha pack, on the website, in the app, the guide and the README.
added: **Founding Members**: the first 100 members keep a Founding Member badge, their name in the credits (Help >
  About, the website's Members page) and their price for good; the membership page counts the places left
  (website/founding.json).
changed: The membership card: the full card shows on the first start only (and from Help > Support); after that a
  small card along the bottom of the canvas comes at a good moment (about 20 minutes of active sculpting in a
  session, a Save Render, the first start after an update, the monthly challenge's results), at most once a week,
  never mid-stroke or during a job. **Become a member**, **Not now** (a week) and **I'm already a member**, which
  signs in with GitHub like member builds: a member never sees it again (and gets member builds); no sign-in hides it
  for three months. Signed-in members never see it. The 10-hour reminder and the old "Maybe later" are gone.
testers:
  - Any OS: Edit > Preferences > Support > I'm already a member: the GitHub code shows in a card along the bottom of
    the canvas; Cancel closes it, and the status bar says no membership cards for three months.
  - Any OS, a member with the sculptforge-insiders invitation: I'm already a member, enter the code on GitHub: the
    card closes with a thank-you, Preferences > Updates shows "Signed in as ...", Preferences > Support thanks you.
  - Phone and desktop browser: sculptforge.app/support.html shows the Founding Member note with "N of 100 left" and
    the Member Requests section; nothing overlaps, no sideways scrolling.
