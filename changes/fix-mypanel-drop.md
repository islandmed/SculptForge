whats_new: **My Panel** takes an Alt+drag however it is open: from the **Edit** menu, in a tray, or in a window of its own (before, only when opened with its key).
fixed: **My Panel** (reported by Hameron on Discord): holding Alt and dragging a button or a section onto My Panel only worked when it was opened with its key. Opened from the Edit menu, the press closed the drop-down before the drop; in a window of its own, the main window's drag never reached it. Both take the drop now (an own window adds at the end of the panel).
testers:
  - Edit > My Panel..., then hold Alt and drag a shelf button onto it: the drop-down stays and adds it.
  - Put My Panel in a window of its own (its tray icon > Open in its own window), Alt+drag a button from the main window onto it: it is added at the end, the status says so.
