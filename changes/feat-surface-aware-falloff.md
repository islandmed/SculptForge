whats_new: "**Contact facing**: a new brush falloff option. The surface under the cursor decides what moves, so the far wall of a narrow slot or the other side of a fold stays put while you sculpt one side. Reported on Discord."
added: "**Contact facing** (Sculpt > Brush, beside Connected only and View falloff; off at first): the falloff is measured along the surface, so what a brush reaches only round a fold stays put, and surface turned away from the one under the cursor fades past half of the **Facing angle** (180 at first). A stroke along the edge of a narrow slot no longer moves the opposite wall (Standard, Clay, Smooth, Inflate: none of it, where it moved 82 to 100% as far as the side under the cursor), while the rim follows smoothly; smooth forms move exactly as without it. On plain meshes and Micro levels (the surface distance on the base mesh). Move and Grab still reach everything. Saved with the preferences and the project; in Classic it shows with Show newer menu rows. Reported on Discord."
testers:
  - "Any OS: a sphere, cut a deep narrow slot (Standard with Alt, small brush, several passes), then a brush about five times the slot's width along its edge with Contact facing off, then on: off, the slot pinches shut; on, the far wall stays and the rim stays clean (no streaks). Standard, Clay, Smooth, Inflate; mouse, pen (varied pressure) and trackpad."
  - "The same on a Micro level (Subdivide > Micro) over a base with the slot; and a big stroke on a smooth Micro sphere looks the same on and off."
  - "Classic layout: the row is hidden until Preferences > Interface > Show newer menu rows."
