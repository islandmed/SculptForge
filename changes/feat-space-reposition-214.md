whats_new: **Slide a selection while you draw it**: hold **Space** while dragging a Mask, Select or Cut rectangle or lasso to move the whole shape; let go of Space to keep sizing.
added: **Hold Space to reposition a selection** (#214, thanks @fxrtst): while you drag out a Mask, Select or Cut rectangle, line or lasso, holding Space slides the whole shape with the cursor instead of resizing it; let go and the drag sizes it again. Works in the Classic and Streamlined layouts, with a mouse or a pen.
testers:
  - Any OS: Ctrl+drag a Mask Rectangle (or Ctrl+Shift+drag a Cut Rectangle), hold Space mid-drag and move; the shape slides without changing size. Release Space and keep dragging; it sizes again. Let go of the mouse: the mask or cut lands where the shape ended.
  - Pen tablet: the same with the pen tip down; also with a Lasso.
