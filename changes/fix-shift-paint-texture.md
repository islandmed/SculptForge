fixed: Holding Shift with the Paint brush (Sculpt off) on a Part that paints into a texture smoothed the shape and dented it. Shift now only ever smooths colour with the Paint brush, on every Part; on a texture Part it blends the texels under the brush toward the texels around them (about a mesh triangle's width, so a stroke smears as far as it does on vertex colours, and across UV seams as if the surface were one piece), with Smooth's Intensity, mask, symmetry, falloff and the active layer, one undo step per stroke. With a sculpt brush (Paint off) Shift still smooths the shape. Reported by a member.

Reviewers: TexturePainter::smoothDab (src/core/Texture.cpp) runs the tile dab in two passes (compute from the texels before the dab,
then write) so a drag keeps smoothing and the result does not depend on the thread count. Colour only (no material channels). 
The blur's reach is the mesh's mean triangle edge, in texels per UV island (so the same on every chart). Each texel averages its island's texels (box blur from per-island summed-area tables) and, past an island's border, the texels of the surface that continues there (found through the mesh: the triangle across the border, the same spot along the shared edge), so colour edges running across or along a seam blend like anywhere else; the padding ring around islands is rewritten from the nearest island texel. Corner wedges copy the nearest padded texel. Floor (4 threads, 4K texture, 1.3M triangles): 2.9 ms per dab with a small brush, 15 ms with a huge one; the stroke's first dab 40-50 ms (UV islands and border partners worked out once per layout and cached). UiTest values texture_rough and moved_exact added.
