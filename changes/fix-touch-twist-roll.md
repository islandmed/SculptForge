whats_new: **Two-finger twist** now rolls the view with your fingers, is ignored under Turntable and the other rotation locks, and **Preferences > Input > Invert twist** flips it.
fixed: Two-finger twist on a touch screen or trackpad rolled the view against the fingers and ignored the rotation lock (Turntable, X axis, Z axis); it now follows the fingers and does nothing under a lock. New **Invert twist** option (Preferences > Input, off by default), reported by ebarranco.
testers:
  - Android or any touch screen: twist two fingers clockwise on the model; the model turns clockwise with them.
  - Same with View > Orbit lock set to Y (Turntable): twisting does nothing, pinching still zooms.
  - Preferences > Input > Invert twist: the roll goes the other way.
