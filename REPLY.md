# Replies (drafts; post after the 0.10.9 release)

**GitHub #101 (lostpackets)**
Cut is in 0.10.9: one tool with Line, Lasso, Rectangle and Polyline shapes. Hold Ctrl+Shift and pick a Cut shape (or open Tool > Cut). Away cuts through the Part and closes it with a flat cap, In cuts a flat-bottomed pocket, and Add raises the shape out of the surface. The result stays watertight and follows symmetry. Guide: Cut (Help > User Guide). Please reopen if something you use trimming for is missing.
