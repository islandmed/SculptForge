Thanks for the idea, it's in 0.10.9: **the stroke popup**. Press **G** (J in the Blender keys) or the new button under the Stroke tile and a plain grid opens right at the cursor: Dots, Drag Stamp, Slide Stamp, Spray and Lazy Stroke on top, and Add / Sub, Smooth, Mask and Select below, with big icons and labels.

- Hold G, point, let go: picked. Or tap G and click. Works with a hovering pen too.
- Smooth, Mask and Select **stay on** without holding Shift or Ctrl, until you pick them again or pick another brush. A small label by the cursor shows which one is on, so no more Ctrl+Shift / Alt juggling mid-stroke.
- The key can be changed in Preferences > Hotkeys.
