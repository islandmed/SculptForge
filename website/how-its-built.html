How SculptForge is built

A small team. A crew of AI agents. Artists steering.

Built by Artists, for Artists. Here is how the work gets done, and who decides what.

01

The Maestro of Agents

A small team directs a crew of AI agents, like a conductor leading an orchestra.

The agents plan, write code, test and review. People set the direction and make the calls that matter. AI helps; artists lead.

02

Artists steer what gets built

  • Member RequestsOne guaranteed request a month, built first and credited to you.
  • The monthly voteMembers choose what comes next.
  • Testers on real hardwarePreview builds are tried on real machines before they ship.
  • Reporters, thanked by nameBug reports are credited in the release notes.

See the feature queue · How member requests work

03

Every change passes gates people set

  • Automated testsThey run on Windows, macOS and Linux before a change merges.
  • Code reviewEvery change is read before it merges.
  • Security reviewRisky parts, such as file formats and anything that touches the network, get a closer look.
  • Artist QA on brush feelReal strokes with varied pressure, judged by how they feel and look.
04

A release train that keeps moving

New versions land most days, and bug fixes sooner.

Whoever reported a bug hears back in their thread, with the version that fixes it.

Everything that changed →

05

People decide the serious things

Agents propose. A person decides.

Anything about money, legal terms or security is a human call. Nothing in those areas ships on an agent's say-so.

06

AI helps build it. In the app, AI is your choice.

AI agents help build SculptForge. The AI features inside the app are the artist's choice.

  • One switchPreferences > AI > Enable AI features. The first run asks too.
  • Off means offBrush Lab and Generate 3D are hidden, and no AI request is made.
  • Studios can lock itWith SF_AI=off, --no-ai or the policy file.
  • Only when you use itWith your own provider. Your art is never uploaded or used for training.
