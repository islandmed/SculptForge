Glossary

This chapter maps ZBrush terms to their SculptForge equivalents, so you can find a familiar tool quickly, and explains the terms that only SculptForge uses. Features use SculptForge's own names (Parts, Forge Spheres, Vertex Paint, Library...); the short list is in Coming from ZBrush, and the command palette (F3) finds every feature by its ZBrush name too. "Not yet" means SculptForge doesn't have it today; see the roadmap for what is planned.

ZBrush terms in SculptForge#

ZBrushSculptForge
AlphaAlpha: built-in round, square and noise, or any grayscale image. Left shelf, Sculpt > Alpha. See Sculpting
Auto GroupsTool > Face Groups > By pieces (polygroup_by_connectivity)
BPR renderNot yet as a renderer. Headless renders and turntables with sculptforge --render (Scripting)
Brush pop-up (B)Brush picker, B, then type letters (B S T = Standard)
Clip brushesNot yet. The Trim brush shaves to a plane
Curve brushes, IMM, ZModelerNot yet
Decimation MasterTool > Geometry > Decimate (decimate). See Geometry
Del HiddenTool > Visibility > Delete hidden (delete_hidden)
Del Lower, Del HigherTool > Geometry > Del Lower / Del Higher (subdiv_delete_lower, subdiv_delete_higher). See Subdivision levels
Divide (Ctrl+D), SmtTool > Geometry > Divide, Ctrl+D, with Smt beside it (subdiv_divide). See Subdivision levels
Document, 2.5D canvas, pixolsNot planned: SculptForge is a 3D sculpting app only
Draw SizeDraw Size (top shelf, S, [ / ])
DynaMeshVoxel Remesh (Tool > Remesh, remesh). See Geometry
Extract (Tool > SubTool > Extract)Tool > Parts > Extract: the masked part becomes a shell Part, with Thick, E Smt and Double; Extract previews, Accept keeps it (extract, extract_accept). See Parts
Focal ShiftNo slider for the built-in brushes. Custom brushes have Hardness and Tip sharpness in their recipe
Ghost, TranspNot yet. Inactive Parts are drawn slightly dimmed
GoZSend to ZBrush (GoZ-compatible): send to and receive from ZBrush, and import or export GoZ-compatible files (File > Send to ZBrush). See Exporting
Group MaskedTool > Face Groups > From mask (polygroup_from_mask)
Group VisibleTool > Face Groups > Merge visible (polygroup_merge_visible)
Groups By NormalsTool > Face Groups > By normals with an angle (polygroup_auto_by_normals)
Bake, Multi Map Exporter (normal / displacement / AO maps)Tool > Bake (bake): tangent and object-space normal, displacement, AO, cavity and colour maps from a high-poly Part onto a low-poly's UVs. See Baking
Layers (sculpt layers)Tool > Layers: sculpt layers with weights, exported as blend shapes. See Layers
LazyMouseSteady Stroke (L, every stroke type) and the Lazy Stroke stroke type
LightBoxLibrary, , (comma)
Live BooleanNot yet
Local symmetry, radial symmetrySculpt > Symmetry & Gizmo > Symmetry: R (radial symmetry, Radial Count 2-64 around X, Y or Z, combined with the X / Y / Z mirrors) and Local (about the active Part's centre). See Sculpting
Mask by CavityTool > Masking > By cavity (mask_by_cavity)
Masking (Ctrl)Hold Ctrl to paint the mask, Ctrl+Alt to erase. See Masking
MaskLasso, MaskRectTool > Masking: pick the shape, then Ctrl+drag (add Alt to unmask). See Masking
MatCapMaterial: the built-in Lit Spheres, Soft Clay the default, plus Studio (M), and your own with Load Lit Sphere... (load_matcap). See Vertex Paint
Mirror (Tool > Deformation)Tool > Deformation > Mirror with X Y Z (mirror): flips the Part across the plane through the origin. See Geometry
Mirror And WeldTool > Geometry > Modify Topology > Mirror And Weld with X Y Z (mirror_and_weld): the + side mirrored onto the - side and welded into one watertight mesh, keeping face groups and vertex paint. See Geometry
Morph targetA sculpt layer; exported as glTF morph targets and FBX/USD blend shapes
Mrgb, M (material channel)Not yet: there is no per-vertex material channel
Polyframe (PolyF, Shift+F)Wire on the right shelf, or Shift+F
PolygroupsFace Groups: same idea, same Ctrl+Shift gestures. See Face Groups and visibility
PolypaintVertex Paint: Paint brush (P), Rgb, fills, cavity paint. See Vertex Paint
Polypaint layersTool > Paint Layers: Photoshop-style colour layers with blend modes. See Paint layers
Project All (ProjectAll)Tool > Parts > Project > Project All (project_all), with Dist, Mean and PA Blur; respects the mask. See Quad retopology
Projection MasterNot yet. Mesh > Generate 3D > Stamp projects an image onto the model from the view
QuickSaveFile > Save Snapshot (9 in the ZBrush-style keys); the last 10 are kept in the Library Snapshots tab. See Projects
RgbRgb on the top shelf (paint while sculpting)
Rgb Intensity, Z Intensity, Focal ShiftSame keys: RGB Intensity (I), Intensity (U), Falloff (O) in Sculpt > Draw & Tablet and the Quick Menu
Sculptris Pro, dynamic topologyDynamic on the top shelf, with Detail (dyntopo). See Dynamic topology
Reconstruct SubdivTool > Geometry > Reconstruct Subdiv (subdiv_reconstruct)
SDiv, Lower Res / Higher Res (Shift+D / D)Tool > Geometry: the SDiv slider, Lower Res Shift+D, Higher Res D (subdiv_level). See Subdivision levels
SelectLasso, SelectRectTool > Visibility: pick the shape, then Ctrl+Shift+drag (add Alt to hide). See Face Groups
SoloSolo on the right shelf or in Tool > Parts
SpotlightNot yet
Edit > Preferences > Performance, Edit > Preferences > MemEdit > Preferences > Performance: Antialiasing, Fast Navigation, Multithreading, the last stroke's cost and memory. See Performance
Store ConfigStore Config, Ctrl+Shift+I (Edit > Preferences > Config)
Stroke (Dots, DragRect, DragDot, Spray)Stroke types Dots, Drag Stamp, Slide Stamp, Spray, Lazy Stroke (L toggles Steady Stroke)
SubToolPart: same idea, Alt+click to select. See Parts
Texture Map, New From PolypaintTool > Texture Map: New Texture (1K-8K) and paint straight into it, or Vertex Paint > Texture. See Texture painting
Texture paletteNot yet as a palette of texture images. Tool > Texture Map > Import Texture loads an image onto the UVs
Transpose, Gizmo 3D (Move W, Scale E, Rotate R)Gizmo: same keys, arrows, rings and boxes; masked parts stay, Falloff softens the joint. See Gizmo
Transpose AllAll Parts in the Gizmo panel. See Gizmo
UV Master (Unwrap), UV editingThe UV Editor (Alt+U): seams on the model, Least Stretch, Conformal, By Face Groups, Planar and Unfold unwraps, UDIM packing, texel density, trim sheets (uv_unwrap, uv_pack...). Tool > Texture Map > Auto UV (uv_auto) is the one-click unwrap. See UV Editor
VDM brush, 3D alpha, Create VDM brush from the VDM planeThe VDM brush and Sculpt > Brush > Vector Displacement: built-in maps, Load VDM... (EXR), New VDM Plane, Create VDM, Export EXR.... See Vector displacement brushes
Zadd / ZsubAdd / Sub on the top shelf; Alt flips for one stroke
ZPluginNo in-app plugin system yet. Automate with the Python module, sculptforge-cli and MCP (Scripting)
ZRemesherTool > Geometry > Quad Remesh (qremesh): Target Polygons, Half / Same / Double, Adaptive Size, Keep Groups, Use Vertex Paint, symmetry. No curve guides yet. See Quad retopology
ZScriptScript commands: the console (backtick key), script files, sculptforge-cli. See Scripting
ZSpheres, Adaptive Skin, Preview (A)Tool > Forge Spheres: Draw, Move, Scale, Alt+click deletes, Preview (A), Make Mesh (zsphere_make_mesh). See Forge Spheres
ZTL, ZPR filesNot read directly. Convert ZTools with the SculptForge ZTL Export plug-in for ZBrush (then open the .ZTL), or use Send to ZBrush, OBJ, FBX or GLB; save work as .sfproj

SculptForge terms#

TermMeaning
Brush LabThe AI brush creator: describe a brush in words and it builds a recipe you can tweak. Opens from AI Brush. See Brush Lab
Brush codeOne line of text (sfbrush1:...) that holds a whole brush, for sharing in chat or a forum. Paste with Ctrl+V
Brush libraryYour saved custom brushes, loaded at every start
Brush recipeThe data that defines a custom brush (direction, depth, plane, pinch, pattern and so on): values, never code
.sfbrushA file holding one brush recipe
.sfprojA SculptForge project file: the whole scene. See Projects
Cross fieldThe directions Quad Remesh wants quad edges to follow at every point of the surface, smoothed over the whole model. Singularities (where three or five quads meet) appear where the field must turn
Command paletteF3: search every command, brush and setting by name and run it
Community galleryShared CC0 brushes you can install in one click (Community brushes)
ConsoleThe script console (backtick key): every action appears as a script line
Ctrl helpHold Ctrl and point at a control to see what it does, its shortcut and its command
Gaussian splatA 3D capture made of soft coloured blobs (from phone scanning apps). Load one as a reference, make it a sculptable mesh, or turn a sculpt into splats. See Gaussian splats and From scan to sculptable mesh
Falloff (Gizmo)How far a gizmo move eases in from the mask's edge, as a share of the Part's size: a soft joint when posing. See Gizmo
Generate 3DTurn an image or a description into a sculptable model. See Image and text to 3D
GizmoThe Move / Scale / Rotate handles (W E R) for placing and posing a Part or its unmasked part. See Gizmo
LOD chainA series of lighter copies of a model (LOD0, LOD1...) for game engines. See Geometry
MCPModel Context Protocol: sculptforge-cli --mcp lets AI agents such as Claude run SculptForge commands
PivotThe point the Gizmo turns and scales about. Alt+click the model to put it there. See The pivot
SubdivideOne-way subdivision without levels (Tool > Geometry > Subdivide, subdivide); Divide keeps the levels
Send to UnrealExport and import a model into a running Unreal Editor in one step (Ctrl+U)
Shelves, palettes, traysThe interface around the canvas: shelves hold the most-used controls, palettes hold everything, trays hold pinned palettes. See The interface
Studio brush folderA shared, read-only brush folder for a team (SCULPTFORGE_BRUSH_PATH). See For studios
Paint target (Vertex / Texture)Where painting goes on a Part that has a texture: its vertices (vertex paint) or its texture. Top shelf, next to Rgb. See Texture painting
Quad RemeshSculptForge's automatic quad retopology (ZBrush's ZRemesher), in Tool > Geometry. See Quad retopology
Mirror plane (Quad Remesh)The plane Quad Remesh's Symmetry mirrors across: the model's own middle (the world's centre plane when the model is symmetric about it), found from the model. Like ZBrush's local symmetry. See Quad retopology
Padding (gutter)The few texels around each UV island that texture painting and baking also fill, so seams don't show. See Texture painting and Baking
BakeCapture a high-poly's detail as images (maps) on a low-poly's UVs. See Baking
Cage (front / rear distance)How far in front of and behind the low-poly the bake's rays look for the high-poly. See Baking
Displacement mapA map of how far the high-poly lies from the low-poly along its normal; mid-grey = 0. See Baking
Keep TextureRemesh option (Voxel Remesh, Quad Remesh): give the new surface Auto UVs and project the painted texture onto it, so the paint survives. See Texture painting
MikkTSpaceThe standard way of computing tangents that game engines share, so tangent-space normal maps look the same everywhere. See Baking
Normal mapA map of surface directions that makes a low-poly shade like the high-poly. Tangent-space maps are relative to the surface (for engines), object-space maps to the model. See Baking
TexelOne pixel of a texture. Texture painting paints texels; their 3D position on the model decides the brush falloff
UV islandA piece of the surface flattened into the texture square. Unwrap and Auto UV cut the model into islands along seams. See UV Editor
UDIM tileOne unit square of UV space, numbered 1001 (u and v between 0 and 1), 1002 to its right, ten per row: a Part's islands can spread over several, each its own texture. See UV Editor
Texel densityTexels of the texture per unit of surface length: equal density makes detail look the same size everywhere. See UV Editor
Trim sheetA texture of horizontal strips that repeat left to right; islands are laid into a strip. See UV Editor
Antialiasing (AA)Smoothing the 3D view's edges with several samples per pixel (Off, 2x, 4x, 8x in Edit > Preferences > Performance). See Performance
BVHBounding volume hierarchy: the structure SculptForge uses to find the surface under the cursor and the vertices under a brush. Its update time is shown per dab in Edit > Preferences > Performance
DabOne step of a brush along a stroke. Its cost per dab decides whether a stroke keeps up with the display (under 16 ms at 60 Hz). See Performance
Fast NavigationDrawing scenes of 10 million triangles or more without antialiasing while the view moves, so it keeps up. See Performance
MultithreadingBrushes, normal and BVH updates and saving spread over every processor core; same results either way. See Performance
