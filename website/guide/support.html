Support and membership

In short:

  • SculptForge is free for everyone, and nothing is ever paywalled.
  • Members get betas early, vote on what gets built and are thanked by name.
  • The app has no payment code, no account and no licence check.
  • Help›Support SculptForge... opens the membership page in your browser.

What a membership adds#

Every release stays free for everyone. Bug-fix and security releases go out to everyone the same day.

  • Betas and early builds: starting with SculptForge 2027, each release about two weeks early.
  • A vote on what gets built next.
  • Member Requests: one request a month, built first and credited to you. Bugs are always free, and every idea goes in the public feature queue.
  • Your name in the credits, in Help›About.

Becoming a member#

Help›Support SculptForge... opens sculptforge.app/support.html in your browser. That is all it does: payment happens on the website, never in the app.

The small heart at the right of the top bar opens the same page. Members see a small badge there instead.

The membership card#

The first start shows the memberships once. Edit›Preferences›Support›Show the memberships shows them again.

When the small card comes, and how to stop it

After the first start, a small card along the bottom of the canvas may come at a good moment. That is about 20 minutes of sculpting, a Save Render, the first start after an update, or the monthly challenge results.

It also comes at a milestone: your 10th start, 10 hours of sculpting or your 100th save. These are counted on your computer only and never sent.

It comes at most once a week and never mid-stroke. Not now waits a week; after the third, a month; after the sixth, it only comes with an update.

I'm already a member signs in with GitHub (as member builds do) and hides it for good.
