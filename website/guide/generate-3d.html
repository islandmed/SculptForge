Image and text to 3D

SculptForge can turn a picture or a written description into a full 3D model that you can sculpt straight away. This chapter covers the Generate 3D window, the services it can use (free Hugging Face models, your own ComfyUI server, paid fal.ai or Meshy, your own command line, or an offline blockout) and the offline image tools: relief, inflate and stamp.

How it works#

  1. You give SculptForge an image (a sketch, concept art, a photo of a figurine) or a short description.
  2. The service you picked reconstructs a complete mesh: front, back, limbs and all.
  3. SculptForge opens the result as a new scene and, unless you choose otherwise, Quad Remeshes it into clean quads with one subdivision level that holds the detail, ready to sculpt and subdivide.

Nothing leaves your computer until you choose a hosted service and press Generate 3D model. For exactly what is sent where, see AI services and privacy below.

ZBrush: ZBrush has no built-in image-to-3D. The closest workflow is blocking out a base mesh by hand with DynaMesh or Forge Spheres; here you get a starting shape in seconds and refine it with the usual brushes.

Open the Generate 3D window#

Any of these opens it:

  • Drop an image file (PNG, JPG, BMP, TGA, PSD or GIF) onto the SculptForge window. The image is loaded as the picture for the From image tab and the window opens.
  • Open Mesh > Generate 3D and click Generate 3D.
  • Press F3, type generate and press Enter.

The window has two tabs, From image and From text.

Warning: A generated model replaces the current scene without asking, and arrives as a new, untitled scene. Save your work (Ctrl+S) before you generate.

Generate from an image#

  1. On the From image tab, click Choose image... (or drop a file onto the window). A preview and the image size appear.
  2. Pick a service in the drop-down under Full 3D model (AI reconstruction). The default is Hugging Face
    • free models.
  1. Set the service's options (model, token or key, see below).
  2. Choose Remesh and its Resolution (96 to 400, default 256; higher keeps more detail):
    • Quad Remesh (the default): clean, all-quad topology of about resolution² / 2 quads (32768 at 256), then one subdivision level projected onto the model so the detail survives. Generated meshes often have holes and overlapping pieces, so a voxel pass cleans the mesh up first. Step down to the quad cage with Shift+D.
    • Voxel remesh: one even, watertight triangle surface. Faster (about a second).
    • Off (raw mesh): the model exactly as the service made it.
  1. Click Generate 3D model. A progress bar shows the queue position and progress; Cancel stops the job.

When it finishes, the status line reports the size before and after remeshing, for example "Generated model imported (47214 tris, remeshed to 133540 quads (2 subdivision levels)) - ready to sculpt". Quad Remesh takes about 10 to 25 seconds after the model arrives (a 47,000-triangle Hunyuan3D head took 22 s on a busy 10-core computer); the progress bar shows it and the window stays usable.

Pictures that work well#

  • One character or object per image.
  • A plain or transparent background. SculptForge crops the subject out of a transparent background, centres it on a square and scales it to at most 1024 pixels before sending it.
  • The whole subject in frame, seen from the front or a three-quarter view.
  • A clean concept turnaround pose works better than a busy illustration with scenery.

Generate from text#

  1. On the From text tab, type one object into the box, for example "a stylised dragon head with curled horns" or "a cartoon fox sitting".
  2. Pick Hugging Face - free models, fal.ai, Meshy or ComfyUI - your server. Text to 3D does not work with My generator command or Offline blockout.
  3. Click Generate 3D model.

Which model runs depends on the service:

Hugging Face (free)#

This runs each model author's own public demo on Hugging Face's shared ZeroGPU hardware. Pick a Model:

ModelNotes
Hunyuan3D 2.1 (Tencent)The default. Detailed shapes, about 20 seconds, no token needed
TRELLIS.2 (Microsoft)Best detail, MIT licence. Needs a free Hugging Face token
Hunyuan3D 2 mini Turbo (Tencent)The fastest, about 6 seconds
Hunyuan3D 2 (Tencent)
Stable Fast 3D (Stability AI)Quick and simple, removes the background itself

If you never picked a model, SculptForge uses the default, so updates that change it reach you; a model you pick (and save) is kept. If you pick TRELLIS.2 without a token, Hunyuan3D 2.1 runs instead and the status line says "TRELLIS.2 needs a free Hugging Face token (Edit > Preferences > AI); used Hunyuan3D 2.1 instead."

The Hunyuan3D models' licence doesn't cover use in the European Union, the United Kingdom or South Korea. The first time a Hunyuan3D model runs, a notice says so, and it stays in the Model picker's tooltip. See AI services for each model's licence.

GPU time is rationed per person per day, there can be a queue at busy times, and a demo can be paused or changed by its owner. To raise the allowance, create a free token with read access at huggingface.co/settings/tokens and paste it into HF token or Edit > Preferences > AI (or set the HF_TOKEN environment variable). When the allowance runs out you see "Hugging Face's free GPU time is used up for now"; add a token, wait for it to refill, or pick another model.

Warning: The Hunyuan3D models are under the Tencent Hunyuan Community License, which does not cover use in the European Union, the United Kingdom or South Korea. If you work there, choose TRELLIS.2 (free token), Stable Fast 3D or a paid service. Each model has its own licence; read it before using results commercially.

fal.ai and Meshy (paid)#

  • fal.ai: choose a Model (TRELLIS.2, Hunyuan3D 3.1 Pro, Hunyuan3D 3.1 Rapid or Meshy 6) or type any fal model id into Model id. Paste your key from fal.ai/dashboard/keys into API key, or set FAL_KEY.
  • Meshy: paste your key from meshy.ai (API settings) into API key, or set MESHY_API_KEY.

Generations are billed to your account with that service. Errors such as "The API key was rejected", "Out of credits on this account" or "Rate limited" come from the service.

ComfyUI (your own server)#

Runs any ComfyUI workflow on your own computer or a studio GPU server, with whatever models it has. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

  1. Start ComfyUI. It listens on http://127.0.0.1:8188 by default. If SculptForge runs on another computer, start ComfyUI with --listen (only on a network you trust: ComfyUI has no login).
  2. Install the models your workflow needs. The bundled Hunyuan3D 2.1 workflow needs hunyuan_3d_v2.1.safetensors in ComfyUI/models/checkpoints.
  3. In Generate 3D, pick ComfyUI - your server, enter the Server address and click Test connection. A good answer looks like "Connected: ComfyUI 0.42.0 - NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 (24.0 GB)".
  4. Choose a Workflow: a bundled one, or Other file (API format)... for your own.
  5. Click Generate 3D model. The progress bar shows your place in ComfyUI's queue.

Bundled workflows:

WorkflowTabNeeds
hunyuan3d-image-to-3dFrom imageThe Hunyuan3D 2.1 checkpoint (7.4 GB) and a GPU with about 10 GB of memory
text-to-3dFrom textFLUX.1 schnell, BiRefNet and Hunyuan3D 2.1 (about 25 GB of downloads) and a GPU
sculptforge-inflateFrom imageThe ComfyUI-SculptForge node pack; no GPU. A quick blockout to check your setup

Your own workflows must be saved in ComfyUI's API format (in ComfyUI: Workflow > Export (API)). To tell SculptForge where the inputs go, give nodes these titles:

Node titleWhat SculptForge does
sf_imagePuts the uploaded picture into this Load Image node. Without one, the first Load Image node is used
sf_promptPuts your description into this node's text, prompt or string input. Without one, the first CLIP Text Encode not titled "negative" is used
sf_seedSets the seed. Inputs named seed or noise_seed get it too

SculptForge imports the first mesh file the workflow saves (Save GLB, Preview 3D and most 3D node packs work). For error messages and their fixes, see Troubleshooting.

My generator command#

Runs any command line on your computer: a local open-source model, a studio service, or a script that calls a cloud API. Type the command into the box. It must contain {input} (replaced with the image path) and {output} (replaced with the path to write, ending in .glb). Both are quoted for you, so don't add quotes. The command must write a GLB file to {output} and exit with status 0.

The box starts with a command that runs SculptForge's own silhouette inflation, which is a quick way to check that the pipeline works:

sculptforge-cli --set input={input} --set output={output} -c 'image_inflate path=$input subdivisions=2; export_glb path=$output'

A wrapper for your own model can be as small as this:

# my_generator.py {input} {output}
import sys
image_path, output_path = sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2]
# load your model, run it on image_path, write a .glb to output_path

Keep API keys in environment variables that your script reads, not in the command line.

Offline blockout#

Offline blockout (inflate silhouette) inflates the outline of the picture into a rounded, closed mesh. It is instant and needs no network, but it is a puffy blockout, not a reconstruction: fine for simple shapes, not for characters.

Save your settings#

Save settings (the small button under Generate 3D model) stores the service, model, keys, token, ComfyUI address and generator command in your ai.json settings file, so they are set the next time you start SculptForge. Keys in ai.json are readable only by your user account. See where settings live.

Quick shapes from an image (offline)#

The From image tab also has three offline tools under Quick shapes (offline). They need no network and run instantly. The same tools are in Mesh > Generate 3D as Relief, Inflate and Stamp.

ToolWhat it makesSettings
Silhouette blockout > InflateA rounded, closed base mesh from the outline. Replaces the sceneThickness (0.2 to 2, lower is flatter), Detail (64 to 384)
Relief > Make reliefA height-field relief: light areas rise. For logos, coins, emblems, height maps and texture photos. Replaces the sceneDepth (0.01 to 0.5 of the width), Detail (64 to 1024), Dark is high
Stamp onto model > Stamp from this viewProjects the image onto the current model from the current view as surface detail, into the active sculpt layerDepth (0.001 to 0.1 of the model size), Dark is high

Tip: Stamp writes into the active sculpt layer, so add a layer first (Tool > Layers) if you want to dial the detail in and out afterwards. See Layers and blend shapes.

After generating#

Generated meshes are a starting point. A typical next pass:

  1. Check the size of the remeshed result in the bar along the top (the triangle count). If the detail is too soft, divide once more (Ctrl+D), or generate again with a higher Resolution. Tool > Remesh has Quad Remesh (first) and Voxel Remesh for another pass.
  2. Turn on X symmetry if the subject is symmetrical, and sculpt the forms with Clay Block, Move and Sharp Crease (Sculpting and brushes).
  3. Save a project (Ctrl+S): generated scenes start untitled.

From scripts and agents#

The same services are available as commands, in the console, sculptforge-cli, Python and MCP agents:

# free, no token (Hunyuan3D 2.1 on Hugging Face, the default)
sculptforge-cli -c 'image_to_3d path=hero.png; export_glb path=hero.glb'

# TRELLIS.2, best detail, with a free Hugging Face token (without one, Hunyuan3D 2.1 runs and says so)
HF_TOKEN=hf_... sculptforge-cli -c 'image_to_3d path=hero.png model=trellis-2; export_glb path=hero.glb'

# voxel remesh instead of the default Quad Remesh
sculptforge-cli -c 'image_to_3d path=hero.png remesh=voxel; export_glb path=hero.glb'

# fal.ai, keeping the raw (not remeshed) result
FAL_KEY=... sculptforge-cli -c 'image_to_3d path=hero.png service=fal model=fal-ai/trellis-2 remesh=false; export_glb path=hero.glb'

# text to 3D on your own ComfyUI, repeatable with a seed
sculptforge-cli -c 'text_to_3d prompt="a carved stone owl" service=comfyui url=http://gpu-box:8188 seed=42; export_usd path=owl.usdz'

# is the ComfyUI server up?
sculptforge-cli -c 'comfyui_check'

# offline tools
sculptforge-cli -c 'image_relief path=logo.png depth=0.08; export_stl path=logo.stl'
sculptforge-cli -c 'image_inflate path=sketch.png thickness=0.7; save_project path=blockout.sfproj'
CommandKey parameters
image_to_3dpath (required), service (huggingface, fal, meshy, comfyui, command, inflate), model, workflow, url, seed, remesh (quad, the default; voxel; false), resolution (64 to 512, default 256), levels (quad: 0 to 2 subdivision levels, default 1)
text_to_3dprompt (required), service (huggingface, fal, meshy, comfyui), model, workflow, url, seed, remesh, resolution, levels
image_reliefpath (required), depth (default 0.12), resolution (default 384), invert
image_inflatepath (required), thickness (default 1), resolution (default 160), subdivisions (default 1)
image_projectpath (required), depth (default 0.03), invert. Projects from the front (+Z)
comfyui_checkurl

Without service=, commands use the settings you saved in the app. See Scripting and automation.

Privacy and model licences#

  • Your art is never uploaded or used for training. AI features run only when you use them and can be turned off. Off (Edit > Preferences > AI, or SF_AI=off) hides Generate 3D; Relief, Inflate and Stamp stay. See Turning AI off.
  • SculptForge has no servers of its own and no telemetry. A hosted service receives only the prepared image (or your text) and your key or token, if any.
  • The free Hugging Face option runs the model authors' own demos; SculptForge does not host or bundle any model.
  • Each model has its own licence: TRELLIS.2 is MIT; Hunyuan3D models use the Tencent Hunyuan Community License (not for use in the EU, UK or South Korea); Stable Fast 3D uses the Stability AI Community License (free under US $1M annual revenue). Read the licence before commercial use.
  • With ComfyUI, the picture goes only to the server address you entered.

The full table of what is sent where is in docs/ai-services.md in the SculptForge repository.
