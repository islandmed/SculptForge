Support SculptForge

Free for everyone, kept going by you.

Thank you! Your support means a lot. Check your email for the receipt; Insider invites go out within a couple of days.

Give once

Members get private channels on Discord: join the SculptForge community and tell us your membership after checkout.

Insider builds will be shared through a private GitHub repository: give your GitHub username at checkout and you'll get an invite before SculptForge 1.0, when early access starts. Supporter Packs become free for everyone after three months, and each Insider release becomes the public release about two weeks later. Manage or cancel your membership.

Payments are handled by Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay); SculptForge never sees your card details. Cancel a membership anytime. Not a tax-deductible charitable donation.

What your support pays for

  • Code signing for Windows and macOS, so installers open without security warnings
  • Test hardware: graphics cards, pen tablets and SpaceMice that people report problems with
  • Development time for the features you ask for on GitHub
  • Running costs: the sculptforge.app domain and the relay that links your phone for capture

Other ways to help

  • Star the project on GitHub and tell other artists about it
  • Report bugs and request features
  • Join the community on Discord: questions, tips and work in progress
  • Share your brushes with the community
