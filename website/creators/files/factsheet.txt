SculptForge — fact sheet
Version 0.11.7, November 2026

WHAT IT IS
SculptForge is a free, open-source digital sculpting application for Windows, macOS and Linux. It covers the
whole sculpting job: Parts, subdivision and micro levels, sculpt layers, dynamic topology, voxel and quad
remeshing, vertex paint, map baking and facial shapes, with bridges to Blender, Unreal Engine and OpenUSD.
Classic sculpting keys, Blender and industry-standard keymaps are built in.

NEW IN 0.11 (0.11.0 to 0.11.7)
- Dense scans draw a lighter preview while the view moves and full detail when it stops, so orbiting stays
  responsive on entry-level machines. (0.11.7)
- Live mode: an AI tool you connect works in your open window, only after you approve it. It sees the
  viewport, turns the view and adds strokes; every step is one undo. (0.11.4, 0.11.5)
- Studio controls: one policy file sets which AI tools and providers are allowed, an audit log of every AI
  step, keys in the system keychain, sign-in with the studio's identity provider. (0.11.6)
- Quick Remesh (Shift+R, or hold N), or after every stroke; open parts stay solid. (0.11.1, 0.11.3)
- Big scans keep up: orbiting right after a stroke no longer stalls, and big brushes on dense scans finish
  sooner after the pen lifts. (0.11.1, 0.11.2)
- Sculpt layers record Move, Scale and Rotate, with a weight to dial them in and out. Vector displacement
  stamps rotate with the drag. (0.11.2)
- Sixty new brushes in six packs, and a Masking panel: cavity, peaks, colour, facing. (0.11.0)
- Industry-standard view keys, saved camera views, UDIMs packed in columns and rows, pen pressure per brush,
  touch and trackpad navigation. (0.11.1 to 0.11.3)
- Signed one-click updates on Windows and the Linux AppImage. Interface in Simplified and Traditional
  Chinese. (0.11.1 to 0.11.5)

HONEST LIMITS
- The biggest brushes on very dense meshes still trail the pen on every machine we test, fast desktops
  included: the stroke lands, but late.
- On entry-level machines a dense scan shows a lighter preview while you orbit; full detail returns when
  the view rests.
- Opening a scan with micro levels takes seconds, not an instant.
- The very largest scans need more memory than an 8 GB laptop with integrated graphics has.
- Our entry-level results come from a simulated low-end budget on our own machines. Results from real
  integrated-graphics laptops are welcome.
- SculptForge is pre-1.0: frequent releases, and the occasional rough edge. Report them on GitHub or Discord.
- AI features use a tool or provider you choose and connect yourself. Nothing AI runs until you turn it on.
