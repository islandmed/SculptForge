SculptForge creator kit: how you may use it

Everything in this kit (footage, stills, the test scene, the fact sheet, the logo files) is free to use in
reviews, videos, streams, articles and thumbnails about SculptForge. No need to ask first.

Credit is appreciated, not required:  SculptForge, sculptforge.app

- Cut, crop, speed-ramp and grade the footage as you like. Clips run at real speed unless their file name or
  the clip list says otherwise; if you change the speed, please say so on screen.
- Use the logo files as supplied (see brand/sculptforge-brand-usage.pdf). Please don't redraw or recolour the
  cube.

  Say what you think, good and bad.
