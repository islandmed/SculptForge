Privacy

Privacy policy

No telemetry, no analytics, no crash upload. SculptForge sends something only when you ask it to, with one exception you can turn off.

The app

This covers SculptForge on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iPad. SculptForge does not transfer information to networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it, with one exception that can be turned off:

  • Once a day at start-up, the desktop app downloads a small file from sculptforge.app that names the newest version. It sends nothing else about you, apart from what any web request carries (your IP address).
  • If you signed in for member builds, it also asks GitHub, with your GitHub sign-in, for the newest member build.
  • Edit > Preferences > Updates turns the check off. The Android and iPad apps make no such check.

There is no telemetry, no analytics and no crash upload: crash logs stay on your device. Your projects stay where you save them; the app has no cloud storage of its own.

Everything else connects only when you use it:

  • Community brush gallery: lists and downloads brushes only while its window is open.
  • AI features: the brush description or image goes to the service you chose, only when you press Send or Generate. They can be switched off in Edit > Preferences > AI. See AI tools and AI services & privacy.
  • Phone capture and the local tools (such as the splat trainer): downloaded or connected only when you start them.

Studios can turn every outside connection off with a policy file; see Security and privacy in the user guide.

iPad test builds (TestFlight)

Test builds of the iPad app come through Apple's TestFlight. If you choose to send feedback or a crash report in TestFlight, Apple passes it to us with the details Apple shows you when you send it. The app itself sends nothing.

Members

Memberships are paid on the website through Stripe, which holds the payment details; we see the email address and the membership, never your card. The members' channel and member roles are on Discord, under Discord's own privacy policy. A name in the credits is shown only if you choose one.
