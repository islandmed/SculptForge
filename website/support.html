SculptForge Membership

Membership: more for the artists who use it.

Welcome aboard! Your membership is active. Check your email for the receipt; Insider invites go out within a couple of days.

SculptForge itself stays free, with no ads, no accounts and no telemetry, and nothing in the app is ever paywalled. Members pay for time and influence: your name in the credits, a say in what gets built, daily drops, preview builds and, for studios, a SculptForge built for your team.

Every release stays free for everyone; bug-fix and security releases go out to everyone the same day.

One-time Supporter: your name in the credits, no subscription. Pay what you want, $5 minimum.

One-time Supporter

Members get private channels on Discord: join the SculptForge community and tell us your membership after checkout.

Insider preview builds and Studio builds are shared through a private GitHub repository: give your GitHub username at checkout and you'll get an invite to the private repository. Every release stays free for everyone; bug-fix and security releases go out to everyone the same day. Manage or cancel your membership.

Payments are handled by Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay); SculptForge never sees your card details. Memberships are subscriptions to SculptForge benefits; cancel anytime in the customer portal.

Where membership revenue goes

  • Code signing for Windows and macOS, so installers open without security warnings
  • Test hardware: graphics cards, pen tablets and SpaceMice that people report problems with
  • Development time for the features you ask for on GitHub
  • Running costs: the sculptforge.app domain and the relay that links your phone for capture

Other ways to help

  • Star the project on GitHub and tell other artists about it
  • Report bugs and request features
  • Join the community on Discord: questions, tips and work in progress
  • Share your brushes with the community
