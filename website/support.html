SculptForge Membership

Membership: more for the artists who use it.

SculptForge itself stays free, with no ads, no accounts and no telemetry, and nothing in the app is ever paywalled. Members get the next version first, a say in what gets built, their requests built first, and their name in the credits.

Every release stays free for everyone; bug-fix and security releases go out to everyone the same day.

  • Betas and early builds: the next version before everyone else (from SculptForge 1.0, each release about two weeks early)
  • The monthly feature vote: pick what we build next
  • Member Requests: one guaranteed request a month, built first and credited to you (how requests work)
  • The #members channel on Discord
  • Your name in the credits, in the app and on the website

What artists say

Betas and early builds come from a private GitHub repository: send us your GitHub username (at checkout, by email reply or in #members) and you'll get an invite. Join the SculptForge Discord and tell us your membership for #members. For studios.

Studio builds are GPL-3.0 like every SculptForge build: the source is yours to share, and there are no exclusive or secret features. What stays private is your studio's assets, settings and network. Studio questions.

Payments are handled by Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay); SculptForge never sees your card details. Memberships are subscriptions to SculptForge benefits; cancel anytime in the customer portal. Membership terms and refunds.

How requests work

  • Bugs are always free. Anyone can report one, and fixes go out to everyone.
  • Every idea goes in a public queue, whoever asks: you can see what's queued, being built and shipped in the feature queue.
  • Members' requests are built first. Each member gets one guaranteed request a month (Member Requests): post it in the members-only #member-requests forum on Discord, and we build it and credit you.
  • Founding Members and Studio members go first.
  • A monthly member vote sets the order for the rest of the queue.
  • Popular ideas from everyone still get built. Nothing in the app is ever paywalled.

Where membership revenue goes

  • Code signing for Windows and macOS, so installers open without security warnings
  • Test hardware: graphics cards, pen tablets and SpaceMice that people report problems with
  • Development time for the features you ask for on GitHub
  • Running costs: the sculptforge.app domain and the relay that links your phone for capture
