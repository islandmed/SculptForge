Short description

SculptForge is a free, open-source digital sculpting app for Windows, macOS and Linux, with keymaps for artists coming from the other app, Blender or Maya.

Long description

SculptForge is a free, open-source (GPL) digital sculpting application for artists, students and studios. It offers a the Classic sculpting keys (and Blender and Maya styles), and covers the whole sculpting workflow: Parts, subdivision levels and sculpt layers, dynamic topology, voxel remeshing and Quad Remesh retopology, vertex paint with layers, normal and displacement map baking, and facial blend shapes. It exchanges models with the other app (Bridge), Blender (a two-way bridge), Unreal Engine and OpenUSD, exports STL for 3D printing, and can turn a phone scan or a single image into a sculptable model. It runs natively on Linux as well as Windows and macOS, with builds for the VFX Reference Platform.
