Short description

SculptForge is a free, open-source digital sculpting app for Windows, macOS and Linux. It sculpts dense scans with micro-detail levels, runs offline with no account, and fits into Blender, Unreal and USD pipelines.

Long description

SculptForge is a free, open-source (GPL) digital sculpting application for artists, students and studios. It covers the whole sculpting workflow: Parts, subdivision and micro-detail levels, sculpt layers, dynamic topology, voxel remeshing with one-key Quick Remesh, quad retopology, UVs with UDIM tiles, vertex paint with layers, vector displacement stamps, and normal and displacement map baking. Three key styles (Classic, Blender and Maya) let artists keep the habits they have. It sends models to and from Blender with a free add-on, updates assets in a running Unreal Editor, exports glTF, FBX, OpenUSD, OBJ and STL, and builds for the VFX Reference Platform. Optional AI tools (brushes from a description, a model from an image) can be switched off entirely. It is developed in the open by Med and contributors.
